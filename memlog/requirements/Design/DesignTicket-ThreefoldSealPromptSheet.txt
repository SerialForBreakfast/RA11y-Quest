Quest prefix (asset names)
===========================
threefold_seal_

Global style constraints (include in every prompt)
====================================================
- Painterly flat fantasy; torchlit stone, warm amber, dark umber, muted slate blues.
- High contrast; readable on phone and iPad.
- No baked-in UI chrome, timers, or readable quest text in art.
- No watermarks or logos.
- One asset per generation; no contact sheets.
- Authorship per root AGENTS.md: LLM or human masters only for shipping art.

Asset list (initial)
====================

1) threefold_seal_bg
--------------------
Role: non-transparent portrait gameplay background behind all three beats.
Notes: vault / archive hall; focal interest in center 60% vertical for scaledToFill.

2) threefold_seal_hub_icon
--------------------------
Role: hub card thumbnail (wide format per DesignProcess — match enchanter/dungeon hub icon canvas).

3) threefold_seal_title_plate_decoration (optional)
---------------------------------------------------
Role: subtle texture behind heading row — only if art-driven headings are adopted; otherwise code-only Text.

4) threefold_seal_niche_frame (optional)
----------------------------------------
Role: single niche frame sprite reused three times with different tints in code; or omit and use SwiftUI shapes.

5) threefold_seal_success_flare
-------------------------------
Role: transparent success burst overlay for seal completion.
