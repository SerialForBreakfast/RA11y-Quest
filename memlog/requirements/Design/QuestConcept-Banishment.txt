RA11y — Quest Concept
The Banishment

Date: 2026-04-19
Owner: Product / Design (Requirements)
Status: Draft
Process: See DesignProcess.md

Skill
=====
Two-finger scrub (Z-shape) — the VoiceOver escape gesture.
Triggers accessibilityPerformEscape() on the current view.

One-Sentence Lesson
===================
Any time VoiceOver traps you inside something, two fingers drawn in a Z
banishes it.

Real-World Mapping
==================
This gesture dismisses alerts, modals, sheets, action sheets, and navigates
back in navigation controllers. It is the universal escape hatch in every
iOS app. Without it, users get stranded inside presented views with no way
out. With it, no presented UI can trap them permanently.

Prerequisites
=============
- Enchanter's Trial (swipe to navigate, double-tap to activate)
- Crystal Resonance (three-finger scroll)

The player must already have focus navigation and activation in muscle memory.
This quest introduces the first two-finger gesture in the curriculum and lays
the foundation for Magic Tap and the Rotor.

Downstream Skills Unlocked
===========================
- Magic Tap (two-finger double-tap) — context-aware action
- The Rotor (two-finger twist) — change navigation granularity
Both build on the two-finger gesture vocabulary introduced here.

Stage Structure
===============

Stage 1 — The Ward (untimed, hints visible)
-------------------------------------------
A single Shadow Rat ambushes the player. A modal overlay fills the screen.
VoiceOver announces the creature and that the Mark of Z banishes it.
The player draws the two-finger Z. The creature flees. A door opens.

One creature. Hint visible. Zero pressure. No rank.

If the player tries double-tap: "The Shadow Rat laughs. Only Fear can banish it."
If the player tries to swipe: "There is nothing to navigate to. Cast the Mark of Z."

Goal: confidence — "I can make this gesture."

Stage 2 — The Lich's Tower (hard timer, no hints)
--------------------------------------------------
The Lich sends a sequence of creatures in rapid succession. Each creature is
a modal or sheet that must be dismissed with the Z gesture. The Lich itself
is a stack of three layered threats — each layer requires one scrub to dismiss.

No hint. Escalating pace. Scored.

Creatures / modal types:
  Shadow Rat       — basic alert (one scrub)
  Stone Golem      — full-screen blocking sheet (one scrub)
  Venomous Wisp    — action sheet with options (scrub dismisses all options)
  The Lich (boss)  — three stacked layers, one scrub per layer

Wrong-action feedback (same as Stage 1, no hint):
  The creature shrugs or laughs. VoiceOver confirms the gesture required.

Goal: mastery under pressure — the gesture becomes automatic.

Stage 3 — Lights Off: The Dark Tower (hard timer, no visuals)
--------------------------------------------------------------
The dungeon goes dark. No visual creature. No visual Z indicator.
All gameplay information is delivered through three channels only:

  VoiceOver:  creature name and "Cast Fear" on arrival;
              "Struck" if player hesitates too long;
              "[creature] flees" on successful dismissal
  Haptics:    rhythmic pulse escalating in tempo as creature closes in;
              heavy impact if the creature strikes;
              success pulse on dismissal
  Audio:      low growl building in volume as creature approaches;
              sharp screech + silence on successful dismissal;
              impact sound if struck

The orb / anchor glyph at screen centre remains visible as the only visual
element — the player has a stable focal point even in darkness.

Goal: internalisation — the player proves the skill requires no visual support.
This stage demonstrates that VoiceOver + haptics + audio is a complete
information system.

Wrong-Action Behaviour
======================
This quest explicitly teaches that prior strategies (double-tap, swipe) do not
work inside a trapped state. Wrong-action feedback must be immediate, thematic,
and non-punishing:

  Wrong gesture:  creature mocks the attempt; VoiceOver names the correct gesture
  Hesitation:     creature strikes; short cooldown; player can immediately retry
  Cooldown:       minimal — never longer than the haptic + audio cue duration;
                  the player must be able to retry within two seconds

Ranks (Stage 2 and Stage 3 scored separately)
=============================================
  Legendary:  no strikes received, fast completion
  Skilled:    one strike or slightly over threshold time
  Novice:     completed with multiple strikes
  Defeated:   timer expired

Thematic Rank Copy (examples)
------------------------------
  Legendary:  "The dungeon falls silent. The creatures will not return."
  Skilled:    "They fled. The Ward holds."
  Novice:     "You survived. Practice the Mark until it is instinct."
  Defeated:   "The darkness wins this round. Try again."

Acceptance Criteria
===================
- The quest concept names exactly one skill and one lesson.
- Three stages defined: practice / trial / lights off.
- Wrong-action behaviour specified for each stage.
- Prerequisites and downstream skills identified.
- No overlap with Enchanter's Trial or Crystal Resonance skill content.
