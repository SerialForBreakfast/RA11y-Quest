Crystal Resonance — generative image prompt template (RA11y)
=============================================================
Date: 2026-04-21
Purpose: Copy, adapt, or prepend these blocks when using LLMs or diffusion tools
to create **initial** PNGs for the iOS asset catalog. One generation = one asset.
This file is only **generation prompt** text — not Xcode or CI instructions.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
GLOBAL — prepend to EVERY sprite / glyph generation
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Style: Painterly fantasy, torchlit stone dungeon, warm amber light, dark umber
and slate shadows, subtle gold magical accents. High contrast, readable at small
size on phone. One subject only, centered in frame.

Output: PNG with **true transparency** everywhere except the painted subject.
The area outside the subject must be **fully transparent** (not white, not
light grey, not checkerboard, not soft fog filling the canvas).

Forbidden in every prompt: text, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks, UI chrome,
multiple unrelated objects, sprite sheets, contact sheets, before/after grids.

Negative hints to add when the tool supports them: white background, grey
background, parchment mat, drop shadow as a separate layer, vignette on the
whole canvas, film grain across transparent pixels, low-resolution export.

Wide canvas: landscape aspect (~1376×768 or 16:9) is fine; keep the **subject
visually centered** so SwiftUI can center-crop to a square or oval frame.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
MOONSTONE (dungeon_target_moonstone) — lane target glyph
----------------------------------------------------------------------
A single rough-cut magical moonstone crystal, cool violet-blue inner glow,
faceted silhouette, slightly elongated oval footprint. Centered. Transparent
background only. No pedestal, no full scene, no dungeon room behind it. Crisp
silhouette at ~96×72 pt equivalent.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
ECHO GLYPH / DECOY (dungeon_decoy_*) — wrong-lane distractors
----------------------------------------------------------------------
One abstract magical sigil or shard each generation (ember-like shard, shadow
rune, sun motif). Distinct silhouette from the moonstone; slightly smaller
visual mass. Centered, transparent background, no scene. Bold readable shape.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
RETICLE RING (dungeon_reticle_ring) — hub framing ring
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Ornate circular aiming ring: engraved gold or brass on dark metal, **donut
shape** with a **clear transparent hole in the middle** large enough that a
moonstone glyph could sit there in gameplay. No solid disc filling the center.
Subject centered; outer edge may fade but **no** full-canvas haze.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
ORB — idle / locked (dungeon_resonance_orb_idle, dungeon_resonance_orb_locked)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Crystal or glass orb for a fixed hub: **soft transparent or highly translucent
core** so content behind can read through; bright rim glow. Idle = calmer;
locked = brighter, tighter resonance. Circular overall; centered; transparent
outside the orb body. Not an opaque flat circle.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
SUCCESS FLARE (dungeon_success_flare) — optional burst
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Brief radial magical burst, screen blend friendly, **mostly transparent** with
bright highlights; no solid rectangle. Centered.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
LANE MARKER (dungeon_lane_marker_neutral) — thin tick / divider
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Subtle horizontal lane tick or neutral marker, low visual noise, centered
vertically in a short strip. Transparent background.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
BACKGROUND ONLY (dungeon_resonance_bg)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Full-bleed vertical interior: grand torchlit stone hall, stairs, arches,
atmospheric depth. **Fully opaque** illustration (no transparency). No UI, no
characters, no single floating crystal — environment only.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
After generation (for humans — do not paste into image models)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Export sprites as PNG with a real alpha channel; background as a separate
  opaque environment illustration.
- If the export has a white or light-grey mat around the subject, fix alpha in
  post (re-export or cleanup) before importing — the app expects clean
  transparency, not stacked “backdrop” layers in code for lane glyphs.
