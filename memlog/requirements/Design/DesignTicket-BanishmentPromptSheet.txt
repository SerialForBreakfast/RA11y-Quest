User Story
==========
As a designer and developer
I want single-asset prompts for The Banishment
So that final art can replace the current SF Symbol greybox without changing the
escape gesture contract or introducing decorative VoiceOver clutter.

Design Intent
=============
The Banishment teaches VoiceOver's two-finger scrub escape gesture. The screen
should feel like a trap encounter that blocks the path until dismissed. Art must
support the core lesson: a creature-like threat appears, the scene becomes trapped,
and the user draws the two-finger Z scrub to banish it.

The art must not imply that a visible button needs to be tapped. The primary
action is the platform escape gesture, not a control inside the card.

Global Style Constraints (Include in EVERY prompt)
==================================================
- painterly flat fantasy illustration consistent with existing RA11y mockups
- same world as the hub, Enchanter, Rogue, and Crystal Resonance mockups: torchlit
  stone, warm amber light, dark umbers, muted slate blues, golden magical highlights
- high contrast, readable on phone and tablet
- simple large shapes first; controlled detail second
- no text, no letters, no numbers
- no watermark, no logo, no UI chrome baked into the art
- avoid collage layouts and avoid multiple objects in one canvas
- one asset only per generation
- preserve clean edges and clear silhouette at small sizes

Implementation / Export Conventions
===================================
- Transparent sprites: 1024x1024 PNG RGBA, centered subject, generous padding.
- Backgrounds: non-transparent portrait master, 2048x4096 preferred (2:1). App uses
  ``.scaledToFill()``; keep the focal “trap” zone in the **center 60%** vertically
  so iPhone and iPad crops stay calm behind the veil + card.
- Hub thumbnail ``banishment_hub_icon``: **1376×768** PNG **RGBA**, same canvas and
  export habit as ``dungeon_hub_icon`` / ``enchanter_hub_icon`` (wide quest-card
  illustration, true transparency outside the art bed if applicable).
- Effects: transparent 1024x1024 PNG RGBA, soft edges, no hard rectangular mats.
- Do not generate sprite sheets, contact sheets, or multi-variation layouts.
- Do not bake the Z gesture into backgrounds. The Z path is code-drawn during practice.
- Do not bake UI cards, timers, labels, buttons, or VoiceOver instructions into art.

Gameplay Scope Assumptions
==========================
- Current implementation is intentionally greybox-first with SF Symbols.
- Final art should replace the threat portrait and background layers without changing
  the trap overlay's accessibility structure.
- Stage 1 uses a ward creature, Stage 2 uses tower threats, Stage 3 uses a dark
  final threat.
- Only one threat sprite is visible at a time.

Asset Set
=========

1) banishment_tower_bg
----------------------
Role
- Non-transparent gameplay background for **timed tower** trap states (and compositing
  reference for darker moods). **Not** used for Stage 1 practice — that is
  ``banishment_ward_bg``. Swift may show a **faint** crop of this asset under Lights Off.

Requirements
- non-transparent background
- portrait-first composition for iPhone and iPad safe cropping
- readable center area where the threat, ward ring, and instruction card will layer
- slightly heavier / more confined than the ward background
- no characters, no creature, no UI, no text
- should work under a dark trap veil

Prompt
- Painterly flat fantasy background of a narrow stone tower chamber and stairwell,
  torchlit arches, old ward markings carved into stone, warm amber light against
  muted slate-blue shadows, darker edges, calm readable center area for a trap
  encounter overlay, same world as RA11y hub and dungeon mockups, no characters,
  no creatures, no UI, no text, no symbols that look like letters, portrait master
  composition, high contrast but not noisy, 2048x4096.

2) banishment_ward_bg
---------------------
Role
- Softer practice-stage background for Stage 1.

Requirements
- non-transparent background
- less threatening than tower background
- visual path or doorway may imply blocked progress
- no creature and no UI

Prompt
- Painterly flat fantasy background of a torchlit stone ward gate inside a dungeon

Quality Control Checklist
=========================
- Transparent assets must be PNG RGBA with real alpha, not checkerboard or white mats.
- Backgrounds must be opaque and must not contain creatures, UI cards, or gesture lines.
- Threat silhouettes must be distinct by shape, not color alone.
- No embedded text, digits, logos, symbols meant as UI text, or multi-object sheets.
- Subjects must be centered with generous padding and no clipped glow.
- Review every sprite over `banishment_tower_bg` and over black for Lights Off.
- After import: ``python3 utility/qa_banishment_png_assets.py`` (use ``--allow-missing`` only while art is partial).
