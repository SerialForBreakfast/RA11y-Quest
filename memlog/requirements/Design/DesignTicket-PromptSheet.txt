RA11y - Design Ticket
Prompt Sheet for Image Generation

Date: 2026-02-20
Owner: Design (Requirements)

User Story
==========
As a designer
I want a standardized prompt sheet for generating consistent game assets
So that sprites and backgrounds share a cohesive look and meet accessibility constraints.

Global Prompt Constraints (Include in EVERY prompt)
===================================================
- flat vector illustration
- high contrast
- simple shapes, clean edges, bold silhouette
- no text, no letters, no numbers
- no watermark, no logo
- centered subject with generous padding (for sprites)
- D&D fantasy aesthetic (stone, runes, torchlight palette)
- avoid tiny details; readable at small sizes

Sprite Prompt Template (Transparent)
====================================
Flat vector sprite of {SUBJECT}, {THEME_CONTEXT}, high contrast, simple shapes, bold silhouette,
no text, no letters, no numbers, centered, lots of padding, transparent background, 1024x1024

Background Prompt Template (Non-transparent)
============================================
Flat illustrated background for {SCENE} in {DND_THEME}, subtle depth, high contrast but not harsh,
minimal detail, no text, no UI elements, 16:9 composition with safe margins, 1920x1080

Game-Specific Prompt Sets
=========================

A0) Hub Quest Board (Hub Screen)
--------------------------------
Background (simon_room_bg)
- Painterly fantasy tavern/dungeon interior background, warm amber lantern light,
  stone arches and wooden beams, subtle torch glow, soft atmospheric haze, no characters.
  Center-weighted composition with a gently darker vignette at edges; keep a calm,
  less busy vertical band in the center for UI cards. Color palette: warm browns,
  deep umbers, golden highlights. High detail but soft focus in the background.
  Portrait orientation (2x master export, e.g. 2048×4096). No text, no logos, no UI.

Quest Thumbnails (Transparent, 1:1)
- Enchanter relic (enchanter_relic_target): luminous magical relic crystal, faceted
  and star-like, glowing icy blue with soft golden rim light, centered composition,
  subtle sparkles, strong silhouette at small size, transparent background.
- Rogue seal (rogue_seal_target): carved rune seal medallion, dark metal with
  purple-green arcane glow, slightly weathered, strong silhouette, centered,
  transparent background.
- Dungeon entrance (dungeon_room_entrance): dim stone corridor or arched dungeon
  doorway with warm torchlight and inviting depth, high contrast, centered,
  transparent background.

Acceptance Criteria
===================
- Prompt templates exist for sprites and backgrounds.
- Prompts explicitly prevent embedded text/numbers/logos.
- Prompts specify output size and transparency requirements.
- Each listed asset in the Asset List has at least one matching prompt.

Useful Unit Test (Process-Oriented)
===================================
- Given the asset registry, assert each asset has a non-empty prompt entry in the prompt sheet mapping.
