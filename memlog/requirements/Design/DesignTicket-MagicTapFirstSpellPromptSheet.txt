User Story
==========
As a designer and developer
I want single-asset prompts for the Magic Tap First Spell screens
So that final authored art can replace the current SF Symbol / simon_room_bg
placeholder without changing the VoiceOver Magic Tap contract or introducing
decorative accessibility clutter.

Design Intent
=============
The Magic Tap First Spell teaches the two-finger double-tap as the player's first
VoiceOver spell. The screen must feel like casting a ready spell from a spellbook,
not pressing a button. Art must support the lesson: the spellbook is open, the
spell is ready, and the player performs Magic Tap to enter the Tavern.

The art must not imply a visible tap target. Magic Tap is a platform gesture
registered on the root scene, not an on-screen button.

All five first-spell states (entry, VoiceOver required, ready, success, help) share
the same tavern + spellbook visual language. The ready state (v07) is the approved
visual anchor — entry, gate, success, and help derive from it.

Global Style Constraints (Include in EVERY prompt)
==================================================
- painterly flat fantasy illustration consistent with existing RA11y mockups
- same world as the hub, Enchanter, Rogue, and Crystal Resonance mockups: torchlit
  stone, warm amber light, dark umbers, muted slate blues, golden magical highlights
- high contrast, readable on phone and tablet
- simple large shapes first; controlled detail second
- no text, no letters, no numbers
- no watermark, no logo, no UI chrome baked into the art
- avoid collage layouts and avoid multiple objects in one canvas
- one asset only per generation
- preserve clean edges and clear silhouette at small sizes

Implementation / Export Conventions
===================================
- Transparent sprites: 1024x1024 PNG RGBA, centered subject, generous padding.
- Background: non-transparent portrait master, 2048x4096 preferred. App uses
  `.scaledToFill()`; keep the focal spellbook / tavern table zone in the
  center-vertical 50-65% so iPhone and iPad crops stay calm behind the scrim
  and instruction panel.
- Do not generate sprite sheets, contact sheets, or multi-variation layouts.
- Do not bake the two-finger hand or gesture indicator into the background.
- Do not bake UI cards, nav titles, buttons, or instruction copy into art.

Asset Set
=========

1) magictap_bg
--------------
Role
- Opaque full-screen backdrop for all five first-spell states (entry, VoiceOver
  required, ready, success, help). QuestPaintAmbientBackdrop renders it behind
  a dark readable scrim; the text and controls float above.

Requirements
- non-transparent background
- portrait-first composition for iPhone and iPad safe cropping
- warm torchlit tavern interior: stone wall, rough wood table or shelf, amber
  lantern or candle light, dark umber shadows
- a sense that a spellbook or magic surface is present on the table, subtly
  glowing or open — but NO large gesture sigil (that goes in magictap_spellbook_sigil)
- readable dark center lane where scrim and instruction panel will layer
- no characters, no hands, no fingers, no UI, no text
- must remain calm as a backdrop when darkened by a 55-70% translucent scrim

Prompt
- Painterly flat fantasy tavern interior background: rough stone wall, warm wood
  table surface, one lit candle or amber lantern in the scene, dark umber shadows,
  warm golden glow pooling on the table, spellbook suggestion (closed book spine
  or faint open page) resting on the table with a subtle magical ambient glow,
  calm readable center area for a text panel and controls overlay, same world as
  RA11y hub and dungeon mockups, no characters, no hands, no fingers, no creatures,
  no UI, no text, no readable letters or numbers, portrait master composition,
  high contrast but not noisy, 2048x4096.

2) magictap_spellbook_sigil
---------------------------
Role
- Transparent center hero for the ready and success states. Replaces the SF Symbol
  placeholder (hand.tap.fill + book.closed.fill with concentric circles) in
  iOSMagicTapFirstSpellView. Rendered at approximately 280-320 pt tall, centered
  above the instruction panel.

Requirements
- transparent background (PNG RGBA, real alpha — no white or dark matte)
- subject: an open spellbook lying on a tavern table surface, with a glowing
  circular or arcane sigil above or within the open pages
- a soft ethereal glyph or ghostly arcane handprint symbol above the sigil
  suggesting a gentle double-tap activation — rendered abstractly, not as a
  realistic hand; two concentric activation rings are also acceptable
- the overall composition should feel like "a spell ready to cast," not a button
- centered, generous padding — avoid clipping the glow
- no UI chrome, no text, no baked labels, no arrows, no speech bubbles

NOTE: Prompts referencing specific fingers or realistic hands may trigger content
filters in some generators. Use the abstract/glyph language in the prompt below.
If the generator still flags it, use the fallback prompt (no hand/glyph at all).

Prompt (preferred — abstract glyph)
- Single open fantasy spellbook resting on a warm wooden tavern table, glowing
  circular arcane sigil rising from the open pages in warm gold and amber light,
  a soft ethereal glyph above the sigil suggesting a gentle double-tap motion,
  rendered as a ghostly arcane trace or faint magical handprint symbol — not a
  realistic hand — painterly flat RA11y fantasy illustration style, centered with
  generous padding, transparent background, no UI chrome, no text, no letters,
  no numbers, no arrows, no extra objects, 1024x1024.

Prompt (fallback — sigil only, no hand/glyph)
- Single open fantasy spellbook on a warm wooden tavern table, circular glowing
  arcane sigil rising from the open pages, warm gold and amber light, soft magical
  glow with small ember particles, the sigil radiating outward with two concentric
  rings suggesting activation, painterly flat RA11y fantasy illustration style,
  centered with generous padding, transparent background, no characters, no UI
  chrome, no text, no letters, no numbers, no arrows, no extra objects, 1024x1024.

Quality Control Checklist
=========================
- Transparent assets must be PNG RGBA with real alpha, not checkerboard or white/dark mats.
- magictap_bg must be opaque and must not contain UI cards, gesture hands, or copy.
- magictap_spellbook_sigil: subject centered, no hard rectangular crop on the glow.
- No embedded text, digits, logos, or symbols intended as readable UI text.
- magictap_spellbook_sigil reviewed over magictap_bg (with scrim) and over black.
- magictap_bg reviewed at 55-70% dark scrim opacity; text and gold panel remain readable.
- magictap_bg focal zone verified: center 50-65% vertically reads calmly under overlay.
- magictap_spellbook_sigil hand must read as "about to double-tap" not "pressing a button."
- After import: build the app and verify both images resolve without warnings.

Generation Order
================
Generate magictap_bg first; use it as a backdrop reference when generating
magictap_spellbook_sigil to confirm the sigil reads clearly over the tavern surface.
