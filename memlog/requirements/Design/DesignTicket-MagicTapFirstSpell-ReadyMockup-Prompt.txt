Primary request (copy into image tool)
--------------------------------------

Use case: ui-mockup

Asset type: RA11y Phase 3 iPhone portrait UI mockup

Primary request: Create one polished iPhone portrait app screen for RA11y first-run onboarding: "First Spell: Magic Tap."

Scene/backdrop: Warm torchlit fantasy tavern interior matching the RA11y QuestPaint style: stone wall, wood table, amber lantern light, dark umber shadows, subtle gold magical glow, readable dark center lane.

Subject: An open spellbook or circular spell sigil on a tavern table, glowing softly to show the spell is ready. The spell object should feel like a ready action, not like a button.

Style/medium: Painterly semi-realistic fantasy mobile UI mockup, consistent with RA11y hub and lesson card mockups. **One** dark translucent instruction panel (single gold border), large readable typography, iOS app screen proportions.

Composition/framing: iPhone portrait screen, no outer marketing background. Full-screen illustrated backdrop with a dark readable scrim. Top navigation/title area, central spellbook/sigil art, **one unified text block** below the hero (not three separate cards or A/B/C panels), one small secondary Help control.

Text layout:
- Nav title: "First Spell: Magic Tap"
- **Single panel** body (one block, uniform body typography, paragraph spacing only):
  "Two-finger double-tap anywhere on this screen."
  "Magic Tap casts the obvious action now."
  "Magic Tap to enter the Tavern."
- Footer link: "Help"

Color palette: warm amber, deep umber, charcoal black translucent panels, muted stone gray, gold highlights. Avoid purple-dominant or blue-gradient styling.

Constraints: Must match RA11y QuestPaint UI direction; readable with large accessibility-oriented text; the Magic Tap practice surface must not look like a normal tappable button; decorative art must leave clear space for text.

Avoid: no sci-fi UI, no neon cyberpunk, no generic app onboarding, no marketing hero layout, no fake giant button target for Magic Tap, no characters in the main ready screen, no busy patterns behind text, no logos or watermarks.
