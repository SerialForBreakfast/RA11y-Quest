The Banishment — generative image prompt template (RA11y)
==========================================================
Date: 2026-04-22
Purpose: Prepend this block when using LLMs or diffusion tools for **initial**
Banishment PNGs. **One generation = one asset.** Full per-asset paragraphs live in
``DesignTicket-BanishmentPromptSheet.txt`` — copy the matching section after this
prepend.

Execution order and import: ``Banishment-ImageGen-ExecutionPlan.txt``.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
GLOBAL — prepend to EVERY Banishment generation
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Style: Painterly flat fantasy, torchlit stone dungeon, warm amber light, dark umber
and slate shadows, subtle gold magical accents. High contrast, readable at small
size on phone. **One subject only** (for sprites) or **environment only** (for
backgrounds), centered in frame.

Output:
- **Sprites / effects:** PNG **RGBA**, true transparency outside the subject—no
  white, grey, or checkerboard mat.
- **Hub thumbnail** (``banishment_hub_icon``): **1376×768** PNG **RGBA**, same as other RA11y quest hub cards.
- **Backgrounds:** opaque illustration, **no transparency**; no characters,
  creatures, UI, or readable text.

Forbidden: letters, numbers, logos, watermarks, UI chrome, sprite sheets,
multi-object collages, baked Z-gesture paths on backgrounds.

Negative hints (when the tool supports them): white background, parchment mat,
full-canvas vignette fog, separate drop-shadow layer, film grain in alpha.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
After generation (humans — do not paste into image models)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
1. QC: ``DesignTicket-AssetReviewChecklist.txt``
2. Fix mats: ``utility/remove_white_background.py`` (sprites only)
3. Import: ``Banishment-ImageGen-ExecutionPlan.txt``
4. Verify: ``python3 utility/qa_banishment_png_assets.py``
