RA11y - Accessibility Training Gamification
Game Spec - The Dungeon Descent (Scroll Hunt)

Date: 2026-02-20 (revised to align with GameRules-MVP.txt)
Game ID: dungeon_scroll_hunt
MVP Skill: 3-finger scroll to reveal and reach offscreen content; activate target once visible

VoiceOver Paradigm Shift Taught
================================
"Single-finger swipe navigates elements — it does not scroll the page."
With VoiceOver active, the single-finger swipe that normally scrolls a page is
repurposed for focus navigation (moving between elements). To actually scroll,
the user must use THREE fingers.

This is one of the most common support questions from new VoiceOver users:
"I got to the bottom of the list and couldn't go further."
The dungeon makes three-finger scrolling the only way to reach the objective.
The mechanic IS the lesson.

Theme / Narrative
=================
A dungeon with many chambers stacked vertically. The relic lies in a lower chamber
beyond what the screen can show. The player must descend by scrolling with three
fingers. Each room is encountered in sequence. Non-target rooms can be examined
(VoiceOver reads them) but are not interactive. The target room is a button.

The DM speaks in all narrative prompts. The player is always "you."

Player Goal
===========
Use three-finger scroll to descend through dungeon rooms until the target room is
reachable, then double-tap to claim it.

Player Instructions (L0 Lesson Card — exact copy in GameRules-MVP.txt)
=======================================================================
"With VoiceOver on, swiping moves between items, not the page. To scroll the page
down, use three fingers and swipe up. Three fingers swipe down to scroll back up."

L0 Includes a Practice Scroll Area
  A safe, labeled scrollable zone appears in L0 so players can practice the 3-finger
  scroll before their first challenge. This must not be skippable — if the player
  cannot 3-finger scroll, they cannot proceed. The "Begin Descent" button appears
  below the practice area and requires (at minimum) that L0 was shown.

Level Structure
===============
L0 — Prologue (no timer)
  DM narrates the dungeon. Lesson card explains the paradigm shift.
  Gestures shown: 1-finger swipe (navigates items), 3-finger swipe up (scrolls page down),
  3-finger swipe down (scrolls page up).
  Practice scroll area: a short scrollable list, labeled as a practice zone.
  "Begin Descent" button launches L1.

L1 — First Attempt (no timer)
  4 dungeon rooms: 2 visible, 2 below the fold.
  Target: Guard Room (depth 2).
  Single scroll + activation to succeed.
  Non-interactive rooms are informational (no button trait).
  Correct activation ends the level.

L2 — Rising Challenge (soft timer: 60s)
  8 rooms: 3 visible, 5 below fold.
  Non-target rooms give a soft feedback response when VoiceOver focus lands on them:
    "Nothing useful here. Keep descending."
  Target: Relic Vault (near bottom).
  Timer visible. VoiceOver announces at 50% and 25% elapsed.

L3 — Timed Trial (hard timer: 45s)
  12 rooms: 3 visible, 9 below fold.
  Target: Ancient Vault (at depth 10 or deeper).
  Hard time limit. Failure occurs when timer reaches 0.
  VoiceOver threshold announcements: 75%, 50%, 25%, 10s, 5-4-3-2-1 countdown.
  D&D timeout: "The gate fell. The relic is sealed below. Trial failed."
  D&D success: "Ancient Vault reached. The relic is yours. Trial passed."

Lights Off mode (advanced training)
===================================
When the global "Lights Off" hub toggle is ON (see `LightsOffMode-Decisions.txt`):

  - Each new level session (L1, L2, or L3 start) picks one room from that level’s pool
    as the target and shuffles vertical order. Room counts, timers, and scroll rules
    match the locked configuration above.
  - Objective prompts use the **current** target room name (not fixed Guard Room /
    Relic Vault / Ancient Vault copy only).
  - Decorative room visuals may be blacked out; VoiceOver traversal and semantics remain
    the teaching surface (see `LightsOffMode-Recommendations.txt`).

When Lights Off is OFF, behavior matches the fixed targets and depth indices in this spec
for the default learning path and for screenshot automation.

Dungeon Room List
=================
Rooms are presented in vertical order (top = shallowest, bottom = deepest).
All rooms have descriptive, atmospheric accessibility labels. Non-interactive rooms
do not have the button accessibilityTrait. Target room is always a button.

Room Labels (Full Pool)
  Entry Hall. Torchlit. Safe.
  Guard Post. Abandoned.
  Armory. Empty racks.
  Well Chamber. The water is still.
  Store Room. Dusty barrels.
  Barracks. Cold hearths.
  Trophy Room. Faded banners.
  Ritual Chamber. Symbols cover the walls.
  Flooded Hall. Knee-deep water.
  Bone Room. Don't look too closely.
  Collapsed Wall. A gap you can pass.
  Relic Vault. The objective is near.
  Ancient Vault. The relic glows.     ← TARGET in L3

L1 Target: Guard Room. Empty. The objective.
L2 Target: Relic Vault. The objective.
L3 Target: Ancient Vault. The relic glows.

Target Room Accessibility
  accessibilityLabel: "{roomName}. The objective."
  accessibilityHint:  "Double-tap to claim this room."

Non-interactive Room Behavior
  Rooms are read by VoiceOver when focused but cannot be double-tapped to success.
  If the player attempts to double-tap a non-target room, VoiceOver announces:
    "Nothing useful here. Keep descending."
  Non-target rooms must not accidentally register as success.

Gameplay Loop
=============
1. L0 lesson card presented. Player reads (or VoiceOver reads) the paradigm shift.
2. Practice scroll area is available; player must interact with L0 before proceeding.
3. "Begin Descent" starts L1.
4. ScrollView shows dungeon rooms. Depth HUD shows current depth and total.
5. Player single-finger swipes to move VoiceOver focus between visible rooms.
6. Player three-finger swipes to scroll and reveal deeper rooms.
7. VoiceOver reads each room label as focus moves to it.
8. At the target room: player double-taps to claim it → success.
9. Non-target room double-tapped → "Nothing useful here" feedback → player continues.
10. Timeout (L2/L3) → session abandoned → no result written.

Observable Signals (Measurable)
================================
Primary
- Scroll progress changes (derived from SwiftUI scroll geometry APIs — see S1 Spike).
- Target room button activation handler invoked AND scroll progress confirms target was reachable.

Secondary
- Non-target room activation handler invoked (optional mistake, based on S1 findings).
- Elapsed time (monotonic clock).
- Timeout fired (fail/abandon).

Anti-Cheat / Integrity
=======================
- No "Jump to bottom" UI.
- Success requires BOTH:
  1. Scroll progress confirming the target region is reachable/visible.
  2. Target room activation.
- If scroll progress is not confirming target visibility, activation is ignored.

Note on Detection
=================
We do not detect the exact gesture used to scroll. We observe the OUTCOME:
did the scroll position change such that the target is now reachable?
Validation is scroll-progress-based. See GameSpec validated approach from S1 Spike
(TICKET-S1-Spike-ScrollObservability in TicketBreakdown.txt).

Execution Gate Before Full M7 Build
===================================
Before implementing full L0-L3 flow, run this gate check on device or simulator with VoiceOver ON:

1. Start a minimal scroll scene with a tracked target row frame.
2. Perform VoiceOver 3-finger scroll gestures.
3. Confirm `onScrollGeometryChange` updates and `targetIsReachable` transitions false -> true
   only when the target row enters the visible rect.
4. If this fails, stop feature build and revisit detection strategy before adding level logic.

Scoring
=======
Metrics
- timeSeconds: from "Begin Descent" tap to target activation (or timeout).
- mistakes:
  - +1 per wrong room activation (if non-target button double-tapped).
  - +1 per timeout bucket exceeded (bucket: 15s; see below).
  - Optional: +1 per excessive wrong-direction scroll (only if reliably detectable per S1).

Timeout Bucket
--------------
- Bucket size: 15 seconds.
- Each full 15s bucket beyond the first adds +1 mistake.
  Example: 0–15s → +0; 15–30s → +1; 30–45s → +2.
  Tunable post-playtest.

Rank Thresholds
---------------
  Legendary (Perfect): mistakes == 0 AND timeSeconds <= 15
  Skilled   (Good):    mistakes <= 1 AND timeSeconds <= 30
  Novice    (Ok):      success AND timeSeconds <= 60
  Defeated  (Failed):  timeout at 60s OR mistakes >= 6

Game-Specific Result Announcements
===================================
These are displayed on the shared results screen after L3 completes.
The SharedGameResultScreen accepts a game-specific announcement string.

  Legendary: "Legendary descent. No misstep. The dungeon bows."
  Skilled:   "Skilled explorer. The vault yielded to you."
  Novice:    "You found the vault. The dungeon was not kind."
  Defeated:  "The dungeon sealed. Rest. Then try again."

Failure States + Mitigations
=============================
- Player doesn't know how to 3-finger scroll:
  - L0 practice area must be clearly explained before L1 begins.
  - Optional hint button (no penalty): "Use three fingers to scroll downward."
- Player is stuck at the bottom of visible content:
  - Hint should specifically address the 3-finger scroll behavior.
- Provide "Try Again" action to reset the current level.

Accessibility Requirements
===========================
- ScrollView: labeled as a scroll container with 3-finger scroll hint.
  accessibilityLabel: "Dungeon scroll list. Scroll to descend."
  accessibilityHint:  "Use three fingers to scroll and reveal deeper rooms."
- Depth HUD: announced as a group (depth: {current} of {total}).
- Target room: button trait, distinct label, hint to double-tap.
- Non-target rooms: NO button trait; no activation-success handler.
- Timer group: single accessibility element; NOT updated every second.
  Announced only at defined thresholds (see GameRules-MVP.txt timer spec).
- ScrollView must surface the standard iOS scrollable indicator trait so VoiceOver
  can inform the user that more content exists below.

Unit-Testable Logic
====================

Requirement-to-Test Mapping (Implementation Contract)
=====================================================
R1: L0 teaches the 3-finger scroll paradigm and exposes practice zone before play starts.
  - UI test: L0 renders lesson copy, practice zone exists, and "Begin Descent" is disabled
    until a practice scroll interaction occurs.

R2: Target cannot be activated until reachable by scroll progress.
  - Unit test: target reachability remains false for pre-threshold geometry samples.
  - UI test: activation on target row before reachability does not complete level.

R3: Non-target interactions never complete the level and provide feedback.
  - Unit test: non-target activation path does not emit success transition.
  - UI test: double-tap non-target announces/prints "Nothing useful here. Keep descending."

R4: Timer thresholds announce only at specified points (no spam).
  - Unit test: timer announcer emits events exactly at configured thresholds.
  - UI test: L2 and L3 runs surface threshold announcements and no per-second announcements.

R5: Result persistence and best-result comparison remain stable.
  - Unit test: better result overwrites; worse result does not overwrite.
  - UI test: after completion, hub rank reflects persisted best result for scroll-hunt game ID.
