RA11y - Accessibility Training Gamification
Game Spec - Scroll Hunt (SwiftUI)

Date: 2026-02-20
MVP Skill: 3-finger scroll outcome (scroll to reveal offscreen content) + activation

Player Goal
===========
Scroll until a hidden target becomes reachable/visible, then activate it.

Player Instructions (VoiceOver)
===============================
- “Use three fingers to scroll down until you find the hidden item. Then double-tap it.”
Note: We do not detect the exact gesture; we validate the scroll outcome.

Theme / Narrative
=================
- "Dungeon Crawl" simulator: scroll deeper into the training dungeon to find the objective (Training Academy).

Art / Assets
============
- Follow Phase1-GameSpecs-Overview: flat, high-contrast, vector-like; no text in images; sprites usable at small sizes. Accessibility quality bar and asset dimensions apply.

Gameplay Loop
=============
1) Game shows a ScrollView with content longer than the viewport.
2) Target exists below the fold and cannot be reached without scrolling.
3) Player scrolls until the target is reachable and activates it.
4) Game ends on success or timeout.

Observable Signals (Measurable)
===============================
Primary
- Scroll progress changes (derived from SwiftUI scroll geometry APIs).
- Target activation handler invoked (success).

Secondary
- Wrong activation of decoy targets (mistake).
- Optional: wrong-direction scroll beyond threshold (if measurable).
- Elapsed time (monotonic clock).
- Timeout fired (fail).

Scoring
=======
Metrics
- timeSeconds: from “Start” to success (or timeout).
- mistakes:
  - +1 per wrong activation of decoys.
  - +1 per timeout bucket exceeded (see below).
  - Optional: +1 if wrong-direction scroll exceeds a threshold (only if reliably measurable).

Timeout bucket (initial)
------------------------
- Bucket size: 15 seconds
- Each full bucket elapsed beyond the first bucket adds +1 mistake.

Rank Thresholds (Initial Guess; Tunable)
========================================
- Perfect: mistakes == 0 AND timeSeconds <= 15
- Good: mistakes <= 1 AND timeSeconds <= 30
- Ok: success AND timeSeconds <= 60
- Failed: timeout at 60s OR mistakes >= 6

Anti-cheat / Integrity
======================
- No “Jump to target” UI.
- Success requires:
  - scroll progress to indicate target region is reachable/visible AND
  - target activation.

Failure States + Mitigations
============================
- Player doesn’t know how to scroll:
  - Optional hint explains 3-finger scrolling.
- Provide “Restart” action.

Accessibility Requirements
==========================
- ScrollView must be clearly indicated as scrollable by VO.
- Target must be a standard accessible control with label + hint.

Unit-testable Logic
===================
- rankFor(timeSeconds:mistakes:) -> Rank
- bucketMistakes(timeSeconds:) -> Int
- bestResult(lhs:rhs:) -> Result
- progressMapping(scrollGeometry:) -> DerivedProgress (if represented as pure derived value)
